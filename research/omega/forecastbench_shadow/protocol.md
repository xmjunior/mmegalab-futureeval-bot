# Frozen shadow design

Designed 24 November 2026 after the earlier OMEGA archive analysis. This is an exploratory reuse of the historical test, followed by a prospective, independent shadow comparison. No historical result selects a specification or changes a production model.

Use the existing immutable binary panel. Read only resolved outcomes from its training split for fitting. Ignore all three archived model probabilities. Group source and exact days to target. Give every family one vote per issue round (mean of its distinct events), then give each round one vote. For each source add four neutral pseudo-rounds at 0.5. Shrink each source–horizon estimate towards its source estimate with another four pseudo-rounds. An unseen horizon falls back to the source estimate, and an unseen source, including all historical market platforms, falls back to 0.5. Keep the fitted parameters frozen through 31 March 2027. Evaluate versus 0.5 on validation and the final six existing historical test rounds. Report raw, family/round balanced and date-stratified metrics. No claims of independent events, transfer to markets or official score.

For prospective runs, issue at most one JSON per official biweekly round from 6 December 2026 through 31 March 2027. Trust only the official upstream GitHub repository. Pin the revision for the question path when it is first read, require its commit timestamp to be on or before local observation and official due-date close, validate the set name and all 500 questions. Read only the official identifiers, sources, horizons and due date. The CLI takes its clock from current UTC and never backfills an expired issue. Save hashes for the fitted model, input and full forecast set. Repeat runs leave issue files intact. Use the organiser's document-level coverage check. Unseen sources receive the declared neutral baseline. The local ballot never leaves the project. No new API spend.

Use a separate score ledger. Score only externally timestamped seals before the close for their round; preserve the first verified resolution seen with revision and timestamp. Exclude events already resolved on or before the issue date to avoid intraday ambiguity. Unresolved, ambiguous, nonbinary and unknown cases remain unscored. The primary comparator is fixed at 0.5. Report mean per-series then per-source, the macro mean of these groups, and by round. Keep descriptive Brier, natural-log loss and calibration on scored cases. Historical automatic settlement checks stop after 31 March 2028, so longer horizons may remain outstanding. Series and market questions may recur across rounds. No cross-round row-count total is statistical evidence.
