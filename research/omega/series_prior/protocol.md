# Frozen experiment: shrink a series-specific reference class towards the deployed source prior

One candidate, CPU only. The research question is whether the track record of the **same economic or measurement series** contributes information beyond MEGALAB's frozen source/horizon prior. That prior is the no-inference ForecastBench shadow, not the full FutureEval bot. Each scored event must have an official binary resolution after issue. No contemporary model inference, community probabilities or live competition content.

Use the official ForecastBench snapshot 86b92ba06ed34d606d633bff3e99af54dd447d3a (2026-11-22). Fit exclusively on the earlier OMEGA train vintage (official 2026-01-02 snapshot; 1,311 previously validated outcomes). Reuse the checked frozen source model from forecastbench_shadow/model.py. For each training series (source, stable ID) average its outcomes per issue round, one vote per round. Predict (sum of these round means + 4 times the original source/horizon probability) / (number of observed series rounds + 4). Four is the champion's existing shrinkage strength. Unseen series use the original prior; there is one fixed specification, no fitting on later results.

Main evaluation uses 10 consecutive issues 2026-01-04 through 2026-05-10, inclusive. Include the five data sources registered for the OMEGA binary archive. Restrict to single underlying IDs and the ordinary unconditioned direction (empty or null). Canonical event = source, ID, calendar target. Embargo all identities ever issued in the five prior training rounds, including unresolved targets. Exclude the **entire** set of targets already present in the archived OMEGA binary train/valid/test rows. Within the main cohort use the earliest issue of each eligible event, fixed before scoring; exclude unresolved events, inapplicable horizons and missing metadata. Close at the official 2026-11-22 snapshot. Count exclusions and coverage and hash every input. This is a new holdout versus the published OMEGA binary and shadow rows; other prior studies of aggregated ForecastBench performance cannot prove total independence. Use validation/test of this remaining sample only for final evaluation. No sample thresholds or series choices may change after seeing outcomes.

Compare on identical cases with the frozen source/horizon champion and neutral 0.5. Secondary external comparator: if the exact archived GPT-5 mini probabilities are available, report only non-imputed genuine predictions on the common set, with separate coverage; otherwise identify the limitation. This is the preselected strongest individual in prior OMEGA training. The candidate must have at least 97% native coverage, positive average Brier gains both by round and by family, 95% lower date-block and family-block bounds above zero, one-sided exact round sign-flip p<0.05, no loss in mean log loss and no deterioration in ECE10 versus the source prior. Report the unconditional, unseen-series and previously seen series results. Dates, families and targets can overlap; the round and family analyses are complementary sensitivity checks. The primary gate alone can qualify an independent shadow observation; a production change requires separate evidence of transport to the actual deployment and promotion approval. Outcomes cannot establish improvements in FutureEval.
